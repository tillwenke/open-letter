“Mitigating the risk of extinction from AI should be a global priority alongside other societal-scale risks such as pandemics and nuclear war.”

The most capable AI models carry extreme risks: misuse for biological and cyber weapons, loss of human control, autonomous military systems, and potentially far-reaching societal and economic disruption. An unchecked race to build ever more powerful systems is pushing safety aside, and no country can manage these risks alone.

With the 2027 AI Summit in Geneva, Switzerland has a unique opportunity to lead. We, the undersigned members of Swiss academic faculty, call on the Swiss Federal Council to publicly advocate for a global agreement built on the following priorities:

  • Red lines. AI poses the risk of unacceptable global harm. Clear limits must be set that may not be crossed.
  • Binding safety standards. Internationally coordinated minimum standards for the most capable AI models, based on their capabilities and impact, with independent international enforcement.
  • Independent evaluation and verification. An international network that ensures shared safety standards and the independent evaluation and verification of frontier AI models.
